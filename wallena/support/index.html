Common questions

The wallpaper is set but does not move on the Lock Screen

Motion is switched on by pressing and holding the Lock Screen — touch the wallpaper and hold. If iOS says "Motion not available", the system did not accept the file as a Live Photo; try creating the wallpaper again.

How to cancel the subscription

iPhone Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Wallena → Cancel Subscription. It keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How to get a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Sign in with your Apple Account, find the purchase and describe the reason.

I subscribed but generation will not start

Open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore Purchases". If that does not help, write to us with the date of purchase.

Wallpapers are not saving to Photos

Check iPhone Settings → Apps → Wallena → Photos. Add-only permission is enough, but it has to be granted.

The wallpaper did not come out the way I imagined

The image is drawn by a neural network, and the same request gives a different result every time. Try describing it in more detail — colour, mood, what should be in the background — and generate again.

Reporting a bug

Found a bug, or have an idea — write to the same address. We read everything.
