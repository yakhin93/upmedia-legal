Privacy Policy

Wallena · effective 27 August 2026

In short: the app collects no personal data, shows no ads and does not track you. Your photos and videos never leave your device. The details follow.

What stays on your device

Your photos and videos. When you turn your own video into a Live Photo, all of it happens on the iPhone itself. The file is sent nowhere — not to us, not to anyone.

Saved wallpapers. The app asks for add-only access to Photos. It cannot read your library; that capability is not built into it.

Settings and creator code. Stored locally and deleted together with the app.

What goes to the server

Only when you tap to generate a wallpaper from a description:

WhatWhy
The text you typedThe image is made from it. Passed on to OpenAI
Chosen style and screen sizeSo the image fits your device
A random install numberRequest limits. Generated by the app itself, not tied to your identity
The request's IP addressAbuse protection. Kept for a limited time, never used for advertising

The finished image comes back to the device. We keep neither the descriptions nor the generated images longer than the request needs.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see or receive your card details.

To credit a purchase to the creator who brought you here, the app sends us an Apple-signed receipt and a random install number. It contains no name, no email and no payment data.

What we do not do

  • No ads, and no data passed to ad networks
  • No tracking across apps and websites — the tracking permission is never requested at all
  • No selling of data, and no sharing beyond the service providers listed below
  • No collection of name, email, phone number, location or contacts

Who else is involved

  • OpenAI — generating the image from your description (OpenAI policies)
  • Supabase — backend and wallpaper catalogue storage
  • Apple — app delivery and subscription processing

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights and deleting data

Everything stored on the device is removed when you delete the app. We hold no server records tied to your identity, so there is nothing to delete there. If you still have questions about data, write to support@upmedia.group.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears at this same address and the date at the top of the page changes with it.
