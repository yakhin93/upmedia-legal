Privacy Policy

Wallena · effective 15 November 2026

Wallena lets you discover, create and save wallpapers without registration. Contact support@upmedia.group with privacy questions or deletion requests.

Photos and videos

Your selected photos and videos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our server or fal.ai. The system picker grants access only to the selected file. Wallena requests permission to add finished results to Photos; it does not request access to read your entire photo library. My Wallpapers stores images and videos locally. You can clear this library in Wallena Settings. Files already saved in Photos must be deleted separately using iOS.

AI generation

Before your first AI generation, Wallena asks for consent to send your description and selected style through our server to fal.ai. You can withdraw consent in Settings. A later generation will ask again. For limits and safe retries, the server also receives a random installation ID, operation UUID and IP address. Pro requests include an Apple signed transaction and subscription identity for access verification.

Our database stores a request hash and technical operation parameters rather than the original description. Generated images are temporarily cached so a network retry does not create another paid generation. Operation records are linked to an installation or subscription identifier. fal.ai receives the description and style; API processing is described in the fal.ai privacy policy and the API customer agreement. Our cache periods do not determine retention at the provider.

Subscriptions and creator codes

Apple processes payments. We do not receive your card number or Apple ID. Our server receives signed transaction details, including the product, purchase and expiration dates, refund status, transaction identifiers and random appAccountToken. These are used to verify access, restore subscriptions and account for creator rewards. Entering a creator code is optional and is sent to our server with the random appAccountToken to attribute the install. Restoring a subscription may preserve its appAccountToken across reinstallation.

Usage events

Our own analytics receives events for launch, catalogue views, selection, generation, saving and purchases. Records contain a random installation ID, timestamp, app version and short properties such as wallpaper ID, style or entry point. AI descriptions, photos and videos are excluded from analytics. Events are linked to the installation. Turning off "Send usage events" in Settings stops new events; it does not delete existing records or disable necessary purchase and quota checks.

There are no advertising SDKs or IDFA requests. Data is not sold or combined with other companies' data for advertising tracking. Supabase hosts our server and database, Apple processes payments, and fal.ai generates images; fal.ai may pass the description to the provider of the selected model (currently ByteDance Seedream).

Retention and deletion

Retry images are cached on the server for approximately one hour, with cleanup every 10 minutes. Operation metadata is deleted after 7 days. Generation counters, including IP buckets, cover the current and previous seven UTC dates. Raw usage events are deleted after 90 days by a daily task. Signed payloads of completed payment events are cleared after 90 days; pending events remain until processed, while UUIDs and hashes remain for deduplication. Normalized subscription, payment, refund and reward records remain for access verification and financial accounting; a fixed deletion period for these records has not yet been set.

These periods depend on server cleanup tasks running. Contact support@upmedia.group to request deletion of server records; an installation or transaction identifier is needed to locate them. Legal financial-record requirements and ownership verification may limit deletion. Uninstalling Wallena removes its local library and installation ID, but does not automatically remove server records or cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions through App Store settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears at this same address and the date at the top of the page changes with it.
